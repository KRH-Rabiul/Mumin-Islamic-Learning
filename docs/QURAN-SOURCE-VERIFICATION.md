# Quran Source Verification

## Quran text and Bengali meaning
- Arabic/Uthmani and Bengali translation data use the published quran-json dataset.
- quran-json documents the Uthmani text provenance as The Noble Qur'an Encyclopedia and the Bengali translation as Muhiuddin Khan, sourced from Tanzil.

## Bengali pronunciation
- The app does not generate Bengali pronunciation itself.
- Published Bengali pronunciation is treated as a learning aid, not a substitute for learning correct Arabic recitation with a qualified teacher.
- Reference: Quran Hadith Bangla publishes Arabic text with Bengali pronunciation and Bengali translations.
- Bangladesh Ministry of Religious Affairs' Al Quran: Digital also provides Arabic/Bangla Quran resources and Bengali pronunciation/transliteration material.

## Surah introduction
- Revelation place/period and themes are based on established Quran study references and tafsir summaries.
- When a specific occasion of revelation is not firmly established, the UI explicitly avoids presenting an uncertain report as fact.
- Surah-specific special virtues are shown only where a hadith reference is supplied; otherwise no special virtue is claimed.
